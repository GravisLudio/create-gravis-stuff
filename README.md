**Create: Gravis Stuff** is a heavy industrial expansion and geological synthesis mod for Minecraft and Create. It introduces an authentic multi-stage geological progression, custom volcanic fluids, refractory metallurgy (*Gravis Alloy*), and the extreme **Tier 4 Energy (Sugared State / 49,152 SU)**.

---

## 🌋 Features & Geological Progression

- **Magma de Toba:** Formed in a Heated Mechanical Mixer by melting Granite, Diorite, Andesite, and Lava.
- **Masa de Toba:** Forged via Mechanical Pressing with realistic cooling chances (98% / 2% cold rock failure).
- **Masa de Toba Densa & Pedazo de Toba:** Compacted in a Basin under Superheated Blue Fire to extract dense volcanic chunks (10% byproduct chance).
- **Alquitrán Volcánico (Volcanic Tar):** Liquefied in a Heated Mixer to produce superheated fluid fuel for Blaze Burners, featuring realistic viscosity slowdown and smoke/ash combustion particles.
- **Toba Sintética:** High-pressure compacting consolidation yielding pure Vanilla Tuff.
- **Polvo de Toba:** Fine refractory mineral powder produced by crushing Tuff blocks in Crushing Wheels or Millstones.
- **Gravis Alloy & Nuggets:** Tier 4 superalloy fused under Blue Fire (Netherite Ingot + Tuff Powder + Volcanic Tar).
- **Gravis Alloy Block:** Heavy bolted industrial plating compatible with Beacons.
- **Gravis Casing & Fluid Tanks:** In progress!
